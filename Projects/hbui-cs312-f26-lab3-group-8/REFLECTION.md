What I learned:
I learned that ArrayList and LinkedList can perform differently even though they have the same methods. Seeing the ArrayList take almost 150 seconds to insert at the front while the LinkedList took less than a second made the the stuff from class seem a lot more impactful. I also got practice using gnuplot.

Most challenging part:
The hardest part was finding a value of N that took about 2 seconds. We had to keep guessing and rerunning it until we got close.

Was the empirical study helpful:
I think it was helpful. Seeing the time go up about 4 times every time we doubled N showed me the impact O(N^2) has, and that picking the wrong data structure can really slow down a program.

Working with a partner:
Working with Ashley went well. We split up running the programs and recording the times. We talked about why the ArrayList was so slow at the front and figured out it was because every element had to shift over.
