How are conditional statements similar and different from those in other programming languages?
They use the same keywords like for, while, if. In bash, the syntax is very different as we use
brackets frequently to enclose the conditionals, and bash uses the "done" keyword at the end
of a conditional statement to tell the terminal that it is done, however; that is very unique
to bash.
